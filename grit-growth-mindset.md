# Grit and Growth Mindset

## Question 1: Summarize the grit video
Grit means staying focused and working hard toward long-term goals, even when things get difficult. Success needs consistent effort and determination, not just talent.

## Question 2: Summarize the growth mindset video
A growth mindset means believing we can improve our skills through learning and practice. We should accept challenges, learn from mistakes, and use feedback to get better.

## Question 3: What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point?
Internal locus of control means believing our choices and efforts can influence our results. The key point is to focus on what we can control instead of depending on luck or blaming others.

## Question 4: What are the key points to build a growth mindset?
1. Believe in your ability to figure things out; 2. Question your assumptions.  
3. Create your own curriculum for growth; 4. Honor the struggle.

## Question 5: What actions will you take to build a growth mindset?
I will practice coding regularly, try difficult problems, and learn from my mistakes. I will ask for feedback, keep improving, and focus on progress instead of perfection.
